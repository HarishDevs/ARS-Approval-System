Summary

7approver tiers (master + department copies)
11internal / standalone approval projects
54projects that have never held a task
~4,800approval requests processed so far
868automation rules, every one read

The approval system runs on three designs:

  • A. Approver tiers (one master project, many department copies). Each top approver (MD, ED, Finance, Valarmadhi, SCM, Sumit, plus a dormant HR tier) has one intake form and one master project. A rule reads the Department field and copies each request into a "<Dept> – <Approver> Approval Asana" project, so each department can see its own requests. The master is where decisions are made. The department copies are only a view of it.
  • B. Department internal, Manager → HOD. Newer single projects (HR, Admin, Recruitment, Finance Internal, IT Internal and others). A request goes through one or two manager levels before the HOD, driven by Asana's built-in approval tasks. Each one is self-contained; nothing is passed on to the MD or ED tiers.
  • C. Sequential sign-off sheet. Price Realisation runs HOD → ED → MD inside a single project.

What matters most:

  • No routing by amount or between tiers. A single approver signs off anything from ₹0 to ₹5 Cr. MD approvals given on WhatsApp are mentioned in the text of Finance and ED requests but never recorded as a step in Asana.
  • The rules don't check who approved. Requesters, checkers and the first-level approver have each approved a step that belonged to someone else, and the rules moved the task on anyway.
  • Hold counts as done. In the ED, Finance, SCM and internal projects, "request changes" moves the task to Hold and marks it complete, so the open counts are lower than the real backlog.
  • Master and department copies disagree. They can show different sections for the same task, every requester gets the notification comment twice, and one of those comments has a broken placeholder.
  • The rules themselves have bugs (confirmed by reading them). On the MD master, a Rejected decision moves the task to Approved Section. In MD department copies, any completed request is marked Approved. The final approval step in Plant Internal checks the wrong person, and the routing conditions in Branding Approvals can never be true. See Rule defects.
  • Many projects are unused. All 12 Sumit department copies, 11 of 12 Valarmadhi, 10 of 12 SCM and the whole legacy HR tier have never held a real request.

Flows

One flow chart per system, followed by the steps in plain words. Red dashed boxes mark where the flow as designed and the flow as observed differ.

Tier flow (shared by ED, Finance, Valarmadhi, SCM, Sumit)

flowchart TD
  A(["Requester submits the tier form"]) --> B["Task created in MASTER · Form Submission<br/>type = approval · name = 'Requester, Nature'<br/>form answers written to fields + description"]
  B --> C["Rule: approval requested from the tier approver<br/>requester added as collaborator<br/>comment: 'Dear sir, an approval request has been raised…'"]
  C --> D["Rule (~2 s): task also added to the department copy<br/>matched on Department; the copy rule moves it Untitled → Form Submission"]
  D --> E{"Approver clicks<br/>built-in Approve / Reject / Request changes"}
  E -- Approve --> F["Approved Section<br/>result comment ×2 · task completed"]
  E -- Reject --> G["Rejected Section<br/>result comment ×2 · completed"]
  E -- "Request changes" --> H["Hold · completed"]
  G -.-> R(["Requester files a NEW form"])
  H -.-> R
  R -.-> A
  X["Approver sets only the dropdown or a comment"]:::gap -. "no rule fires" .-> B
  classDef gap stroke:#c24141,stroke-width:2px,stroke-dasharray:5 3
  1. Intake. The tier's form creates an approval-type task in the master's Form Submission section and fills in the fields. The task is named from the typed Name answer, not from the requester's login.
  2. Approver set by the form. The activity log reads "<requester> requested approval from <approver>". The due date comes from the form, usually the same day or the next.
  3. Notification. A rule posts "Dear sir, an approval request has been raised in Asana by X". A second copy from the department project's rule inserts a project URL instead of the name.
  4. Copy into the department project. A rule adds the same task to "<Dept> – <Approver> Approval Asana". If the chosen Department has no copy project (e.g. Legal), the task stays in the master only.
  5. Decision. Only the built-in approval action triggers the rules. The task is moved to the matching section, marked complete, and gets a comment that repeats every field.
  6. Rejection or changes requested. There is no resubmission path. The requester files a fresh form (e.g. Finance: rejected at 12:03, new request filed at 12:04 and approved).

  • Valarmadhi is a second F&A approver, not a replacement for Prabu. Example: Yamini M's REQ 2224/2225/2228 was rejected by Prabu, re-filed with Valarmadhi, rejected again, then split up and approved.

Rule defects confirmed

MD master: Rejected goes to Approved Section high

Rule "Move Rejected tasks and update status": when Approval ARS = Rejected, it moves the task to Approved Section and sets Approval Status = Rejected. Rule "Task is moved to a section → Complete task" then completes it. So rejected MD requests sit, completed, in the Approved list. Last ran 17 days ago.

MD department copies: any completion is marked Approved high

Rule "Move completed form submissions to approved" (in all 14 MD copies): when a task is completed while in Form Submission, it moves the task to Approved Section and sets Approval Status = Approved. A native rejection also completes the task, so rejected requests appear as Approved in the department's view. It last ran 2 hours ago in Purchase & SCM – MD. This is the cause of the mismatches seen in task history.

The approval checks look at the assignee, not at who approved high

HR, Admin, ASP Internal, Plant Internal, Branding Approvals, Finance Internal and IT Internal only advance a level if Assignee is the expected approver. They never check who actually clicked Approve. Recruitment and Training Department have no check at all. Anyone who can open the task can approve a level on the approver's behalf.

Plant Internal: the final step can never complete high

The manager-step rule hands the task to Murthy as HOD. The HOD rule only fires if the assignee is Sivakarthikeyan S, a leftover from the Branding copy. So Murthy's approvals will never move the task to Approved Section. The project has had no requests yet.

Branding Approvals: routing conditions can never be true medium

Rule "Route approval tasks by type" requires "Nature is Merchandising AND Nature is PoP" (and similar pairs). A single-select field can't hold two values at once, so no request is ever assigned to Sakthivel S, roshan or Joseph Royden F. The conditions should use OR.

ED and legacy-HR copies watch a field nobody sets medium

The section and comment rules in 12 ED copies and 12 HR copies fire on the copy's own "Approval Status" dropdown, which is never filled in. The copies therefore never move (Sales – ED last ran 27 Dec 2025). Only ASP – ED was switched to native approval triggers.

MD master: two rules act on the same "changes requested" medium

Both "Approval Status is changed → Move task…" (→ Hold) and "Move resubmitted tasks to resubmission section" (→ Resubmission) fire on a native "changes requested". That explains tasks logged as moving to both sections. "Move reviewed submissions to approved section" duplicates the Approved rule. A manual-trigger "Copy of Change Value Category" is left over.

Finance Internal: no reject or hold path at level 2 medium

The Rejected Approval and Hold Approval rules only have branches for Approval Form Submission and HOD Approval. A rejection or "request changes" while the task is in 2nd Level Approval is not handled, so the task stays in that section.

Department routing has gaps medium

No tier routes Corporate Affairs, yet Corporate Affairs copies exist under MD, Finance, Valarmadhi, SCM and Sumit. The 259 tasks in Corporate Affairs – MD got there another way. NBD is routed only on the MD tier, so the Sumit routing rule, whose only user is NBD, has never run. Training and Development, Legal, Telecalling and others have no branch anywhere.

Duplicate and broken comments explained low

On the ED, HR and MD copies, the "raised" comment is posted by both the master and the copy. The copy's version uses the token Task > Creator, which renders as a link, instead of Task > Creator name. On the Finance, Valarmadhi, SCM and Sumit copies, the result comment ("…was Approved/Rejected") is posted by both master and copy.

IT Internal: the step that turns an approval into work is misrouted low

Rule "Task Assignment": an approval in HOD Approval whose assignee is not Babulal M should become a task for someone to work on. It moves the task to Approved Section, not Assigned To. As a result "Task Completed", which watches Assigned To, can never fire.

Price Realisation leaves the approver count to AI low

The subtask-creation rule has "Let AI decide the number of approvals to create" switched on. That makes the number of department-head sign-offs vary from sheet to sheet. The ED → MD rule and the MD rules have never run.

No routing by amount high

A single approver clears ₹5 Cr fund transfers (Finance), ₹18.4 L POs (ED), ₹13.4 L POs (SCM) and ₹11.26 L IT spend. Value Category exists only on the MD master and nothing routes on it. Amount is often blank or 0, so a threshold rule couldn't work today anyway.

Approvals outside Asana and shared accounts high

Tasks mention approvals given elsewhere ("approved by MD Sir through WhatsApp", "already approved by MD Sir… CMO Sir").     • Master and internal projects: every rule was read in full.

    • Earlier sections: the flow charts above were first drawn from task activity logs and have been corrected where the rules showed otherwise (see Inferred vs actual).
  • Sampling. Every project's settings and schema were read. Detailed activity logs were read for 3–5 representative tasks per active project, chosen to include approved, rejected, hold and resubmitted cases where they existed.
