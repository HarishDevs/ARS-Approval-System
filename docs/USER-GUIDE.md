# ARS CMS: user guide (v1.42.0)

**Address:** https://approval.arssteels.net. Sign in with your company Google account, or with your User ID (or your mobile number or email) and password and a WhatsApp code, as IT set you up (or either way, if IT chose **Both**). Questions: IT (itadmin@arssteels.co.in).

**On a phone, a tablet or the installed app** you stay signed in until **midnight (India time)**, even if you close the app or lock the screen; then you sign in again. **On a computer** you are signed out after **30 minutes without activity** (IT can change this), and always 12 hours after signing in. Anything you were typing in a form is kept; sign in again and carry on. Notifications keep coming while you're signed out.

What's new in each release: `docs/RELEASE-NOTES.md`.

## 1. Everyone

### Getting around
The space you work in is called **Central Management System**. There are two views; choose one in the account menu (your name, top right) → **View**:
- **Compact** (the default): a sidebar on the left and a search bar at the top.
- **Classic** (like Tally): no sidebar. After signing in you see a menu of the modules; each module has its own menu, summary boxes and its pages as tabs. Use the highlighted letters or the arrow keys and **Enter**; **Esc** goes back; **Ctrl+A** accepts a form. You can also choose a **text size** for the Classic view.

### The sidebar (Compact view)
From the top:
- **+ Create:** a new approval request, task, project or information (only what you can create).
- **Home** and **Inbox**.
- **My Approvals:** New request, My pending requests, My approved requests, Waiting for me, Approved by me, Collaborating, Request templates. If your designation isn't an approver designation you see only New request, My pending requests, My approved requests and Collaborating (Request templates is still reached from New request). A request you open highlights the list it belongs to for you (for example Waiting for me when it needs your decision).
- **Tasks & projects:** My Tasks, All projects, Periodic tasks, New project, Templates, Task with a type (form).
- **Information:** Information inbox, Sent by me, New information.
- **AI Assistant** (if Pico Bot is on for you), with your chats.
- **Calendar** and **Analytics**.

Groups open and close like a dropdown; the group of the page you're on opens by itself and the app remembers the rest. **☰** folds the sidebar into a narrow row of icons. You only see the modules IT gave you.

There is **no Search in the sidebar**: use the search bar at the top. **Every search starts there** (Ctrl K): its **Filters** opens a full search of requests or tasks, and **Saved searches** lists ready-made searches you can open plus your own. Lists and dashboards have no filter forms: their toolbar has **Filter** (a dropdown with Apply), Sort, Group, Options and a small search icon. On a phone, see **On your phone** below.

### On your phone
On a screen narrower than 768 pixels (a phone, or a narrow window) ARS CMS uses its **phone layout**. On a computer nothing changes. Turning a tablet or widening a window switches over by itself.
The phone screens follow the familiar Asana app layout: a big title with **…** (more pages for that screen), flat lists, and a floating bar above the tabs with **Manage view** (the sliders), **Choose view** (the list you're looking at) and the red **+**.
- **The bar at the bottom:** **Home**, **Approvals**, **My tasks**, **Inbox** and **More**. The open tab sits in a pill and shows its name. The numbers on Approvals and Inbox are what waits for you and what you haven't read.
- **Home:** one line on what needs you (approvals waiting, overdue steps, requests returned to you, overdue tasks), four counts that open their lists, quick actions (New request, New task, Search, Ask AI), the first approvals waiting for you, your tasks due today and your requests in progress.
- **Approvals:** **Choose view** picks **Waiting for me**, **My requests**, **Approved** or **Drafts**. Each request is a card: number and title, who raised it and their department, the amount (when the form has one and it isn't confidential), who has it now, when it was submitted, how long it has waited, **Overdue** or **Due soon**, and the status. **Manage view → Compact mode** shows one line per request. **+** starts a new request; **…** has Approved by me, Collaborating, Search requests and Out of office.
- **Deciding on a request:** the buttons stay at the bottom of the request: **Approve**, **Return** (ask for changes), **Hold** and **Reject**. Each opens a panel with the comment box and that one action; Reject asks once more.
- **My tasks:** **Choose view** picks **Due today**, **Upcoming**, **Overdue**, **Completed** or **Assigned by me**. Each task is one row: tick the circle to complete it (Undo is offered), then the title, its projects, the priority (Important, Urgent) and the due date (red when overdue). **Manage view** has **Compact mode**, **Sort** (due date, priority, title, newest) and **Group by** (none, due date, project); groups fold with the arrow. **+** opens Create task; **…** has Projects, Periodic tasks, Calendar, Search tasks and Deleted tasks.
- **A task:** the top bar has back, **Mark complete**, copy link and **…** (start, block, reassign, edit, cancel, share). Below: the title, **Assigned to** and **Due date**, then details, subtasks, comments and history. **Add a comment** stays at the bottom of the screen; tap it to write, @mention someone and **Send**.
- **Information:** the same pattern: **Choose view** for the lists, **Manage view** to search and sort, **+** for new information, **…** for Received and Sent by me.
- **New request:** the steps (Details, Workflow, Review) and the buttons (Draft, Reset, Next) are one slim row each, so most of the form fits on the first screen.
- **Analytics:** the dashboard, whose numbers and period fold into one **Dashboard and filters** row above the numbers; open it to change them.
- **Inbox:** grouped by day (Today, Yesterday, then dates); each card shows its kind, title, message (**See more** for a long one) and time, with bookmark and archive. **Choose view:** **All activity**, **Bookmarks**, **Archived**. **Manage view:** filters (Approvals, Tasks, Information, System, @Mentioned), read status (unread only) and sort (newest). **…** has notification settings.
- **Search** (the magnifier at the top): one box that searches **Requests** or **Tasks**, **Projects**, then quick filters (requests I raised, I approve, in progress, approved) and shortcuts (tasks I've created, recently completed, assigned to others, overdue, I follow).
- **Your picture:** until you upload a photo (Profile → Upload a photo), it is the ARS CMS round icon: blue for approvers (designation ticked Approver), white for everyone else.
- **More:** your photo, name and email; **Out of office** (says if it is on) and **Notifications**; then Work, Intelligence, Tools, Settings (profile, notifications, push notifications, colour mode, sign-in & devices), Support (Help centre, terms, privacy), the Admin spaces you have, **Sign out** (it asks first) and the app version.
- **While a page loads** you see the shape of that screen (grey blocks where its rows and cards will be) instead of the old page.
- **The bell** opens your latest notifications across the screen, under the top bar.

### Install the app
ARS CMS can be installed like an app: it opens in its own window from your home screen, dock or start menu.
- **Android, and Chrome or Edge on a computer:** the first time you visit, a card offers **Install app**; or use the browser's install icon.
- **iPhone and iPad (Safari):** tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**, then open ARS CMS from the Home Screen.
- **Safari on a Mac:** File → Add to Dock.
- **Maybe later** hides the card; **Profile → App & devices** always has the steps.

### Phone and desktop notifications
**Profile → App & devices → Turn on notifications on this device.** Your browser asks once; choose Allow. Do it on each phone or computer you use.
- Approvals waiting for you, tasks, reminders, information and IT's notices then appear as alerts, even when ARS CMS is closed. Tapping one opens the record (after you sign in, if your daily session has ended).
- On iPhone and iPad they work only in the installed app (iOS 16.4 or later).
- **Send a test** checks it. The list shows every device that gets your notifications; **Remove** one you no longer use.
- Choose which kinds come in **Profile → Notifications → Phone & desktop**. Security alerts and work waiting for you always come.
- If you chose **Block** by mistake, the page shows how to allow notifications again in your browser.

### Search
The search bar in the middle of the top bar finds requests, tasks, projects, people, information and pages. It keeps your recent and saved searches; **Filters** opens the full search. A request number works without dashes or zeros (e.g. "pur 2026 123"). **Ctrl+K** jumps to any page.

### Home
Approvers open on **Approvals**, everyone else on **Task Management**; switch at the top.
- **Approval Overview:** cards for Waiting for me, Overdue for me, My open requests and Changes asked of me (click one for its list); Waiting for me; your recent requests (8 per page); on the right your drafts (open or delete) and **Quick insights** (requests raised, approved and the average approval time for the period you pick; N/A when nothing was approved).
- **My Work – Overview:** pick a period (Today, This week, This month, Last 30 days or Custom). Total = open now plus completed in the period; Upcoming = open, due today or later; Overdue = open, past the due date; Completed = done in the period. My Tasks has tabs; tick a task to complete it, or open it to edit, reassign or change the date. Projects show their progress; Latest status updates lists what happened on your tasks and projects; My weekly workload shows tasks given to you and completed each day.
The first page after signing in. Tiles show what is waiting for you, what is overdue, your open requests and what was sent back. Then, in order: **Due soon or overdue**, **Waiting for me**, **Returned to you for changes**, **Your drafts** and **My recent requests** (8 at a time, with page numbers for the rest), followed by your tasks, your team and your projects.

### Inbox
Everything the app has told you, in one place: **All**, **Approvals**, **Tasks**, **Information** and **System**, each with its unread count, and an unread-only switch.
- **Bookmarks:** notices you will come back to.
- **Archive:** notices that are done.
- Opening a notice marks it read; opening the Inbox does not.
- **Notification settings** (top right) opens Profile → Notifications.

The **bell** at the top still shows your latest notices; **Read more** opens the Inbox.

### My requests
If the form has a Priority field, the **Priority** column (after Status) shows it in its colour (until IT picks colours, Critical shows red, Most important and High orange, Important yellow, Normal blue, Low grey; tasks use the same colours); **Sort → Priority** puts the most important first and **Group → Priority** groups by it. Hide the column in **Columns** if you don't need it.
- **Raised by me / Shared with me** at the top, each with how many requests it holds.
- The **status cards** (All, Open, Waiting for approvers, Sent back, On hold, Approved, Rejected, Withdrawn, Overdue) filter the list. Overdue requests are also counted under Waiting for approvers.
- **Search** finds a request by number, title, approval type or the person it is waiting on.
- **Filters, Sort, Group** and **Columns** (which columns you see; kept on your account). Lists come a page at a time.
- **Progress** shows how far each request has gone; click it to see the road. **⋮** on a row: view, progress, history, update and resubmit, raise again, download the summary, withdraw (the request page asks first).

### My Approvals: the lists
- **My pending requests:** what you raised that is still open.
- **My approved requests:** what you raised that was approved: when, by whom, and whether the approval is still valid.
- **Waiting for me:** what needs you (see section 2).
- **Approved by me:** every request you approved a step of, including ones you approved for someone you cover, and where each one is now.
- **Collaborating:** requests people added you to follow.

Every list has the same toolbar: **Filter**, **Sort**, **Group**, **Options** (rows per page, save the view) and **Search**. Filters in use show as pills with **×**. A saved view can be shared or made your default.

### Raise a request
1. **Request templates** → pick the approval type IT set up (e.g. *Purchase & SCM Approval*).
2. Fill in the form. Add files if needed: up to 5 at a time, 10 MB each (PDF, images, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV, text).
3. **Submit request.** You see its number (e.g. `PUR-2026-000123`), and the first approvers are told by email and WhatsApp.

**Drafts:** what you type is saved as a draft as you go ("Draft saved 10:42"). If you leave, open it again from **Home → Your drafts**. **Discard draft** clears it. Files aren't kept in drafts, so add them again before submitting. Drafts are removed when you submit, or after 30 days.

A line above the **level map** says who it's waiting for, since when, and what comes next. The level map shows every level, who approves, and where it is now. Levels that the amount (DoA) or a rule doesn't need show as *skipped*.

### New request (your own approvers)
Three steps: Request details, Approval workflow, Review & submit (on a computer each column scrolls on its own; each approval level is a small card on top, click one to edit it); the bar with Save as draft, Reset, Back and Next is at the top, under the steps. Each level is **Approval** (they approve or reject), **Work task** (they do the work and mark it done; it also appears in their My Tasks, and ticking it there marks it done on the request; untick **The request waits until it's done** to give the work as tasks and let the request move straight on, in which case finishing the tasks decides nothing on the request, and the people can read the request) or **FYI** (they're told and the request moves on; they can open it from the message; put it after the last approval to tell people only once it's fully approved). Fields with a red **\*** are required: **Next** moves on only when they're all filled in, and shows what's missing. The approvers can be changed only after Next. **Submit request** is on Review & submit.
For something without an approval type: **New request** → title, the fields IT has set up (Admin → Custom approval), files (optional) → pick who approves. It starts with your reporting manager as approver.
- **in order:** add a level for each;
- **at the same time:** use *Add parallel steps*;
- **depending on something:** *Add a choice*, only if IT switched choices on.

You can't approve your own request, but you can give yourself a **work task** (e.g. *Raise the PO* after approval). For **Specific people**, type a name and pick from the list. Approval steps list only people who can approve; a work task or an FYI can go to anyone. Options marked "(set by IT)" can't be changed. A request can be **raised again** with the same approvers.

### When an approver asks for changes
You get a message. Open the request, change the fields or add files, then **Resubmit**. It goes back to the level that asked, or to the first level if the approval type is set that way.
If your changes alter who must approve (for example a higher amount now needs another level), it restarts from the **first level the change affects**; levels before it keep their approvals. The history says which levels were added or removed and where it restarted.
An **Expired** approval (past its valid-until date) still counts as approved; to amend it or raise a follow-up, give a short reason, which is kept in its history.
When you resubmit, the same checks as a new request run again (duplicates, unique values, budget): fix what they point out before it goes back to the approvers.

### The road (where your request is)
**v1.48.0:** the request page opens on the **Approval Journey** card: the request's number, title, status and percentage, **refresh** and **share**, the road with the van and every level (hover over a level, or reach it with the keyboard, for its status, time and approver; tap it to jump to the Approval levels card), and a banner when it ends. Folded, the header still shows a small road with the van. Below it: **Request details** (with Priority in colour), **Approval levels** (who it waits for, progress, time in process), your actions, Attachments, Collaborators and Discussion on the right, then Linked requests, the **Timeline** (the latest 3; **Show more** opens the rest) and Reuse. On a phone the journey shows the road and its banner, then Request details, then the rest. Each status has one colour everywhere: blue in progress, amber on hold, orange changes required, green approved, red rejected, purple withdrawn, grey expired.
At the top of a request page, under its title and number, the **approval journey** shows where it is: **Request submitted**, one point per level, and the finish flag. Below it, **Request details** starts with who raised it (User ID, department, designation, email, reporting manager), then the form; then **Approval levels** with the progress figures and each level. On the right: your action box (the decisions as buttons: Approve, Ask for changes, Put on hold, Ask a question, Reject; or recall, withdraw), then Attachments, then Collaborators.
- Just after you submit, your request is folded into a sealed envelope and loaded into the red **ARS van**, which drives to the first level.
- While a level decides, the van waits beside it and the request is out for review (blue point). When they approve, the request gets a green stamp, is folded back into the van, and the van drives to the next level. The finished road is green.
- The page checks for news every 10 seconds (at once when you come back to it); a reload shows where it is without replaying.
- Fully approved: the van reaches the flag, the road turns green and shows 100%.
- Rejected: a red stamp where it stopped. Changes asked: an orange stamp and the van drives back to you; it sets off again when you resubmit. Amber: on hold or stuck.
- **Work tasks** and **Information (FYI)** show beside the road: who has each task and its status (and whether the request waits for it), and who was told and whether the message reached them.
- Hide the journey with the arrow at its top right (remembered on your device). With "reduce motion" on in your device, nothing moves; everything is still shown.
- Tap a point to see who decides there, when, and their comments.
- Below the road: progress, levels completed and remaining, time in process and the last update.

### Withdraw
While it's open: **Withdraw** on the request. The approvers are told.

### Comments and mentions
Comments on a request can't be edited or removed. To tell someone in particular, type `@` and their User ID (or tap their name under the box). Only people who can already see the request are told; if someone couldn't be told, you see their User ID after posting.

### Download a summary
**Download PDF** at the top of a request: details, levels, who decided what, files and history.

### Tasks & projects
- **Create task:** **+ Create → Task** opens it. On a computer it is a card in the middle of the screen: task details on the left (name, who it's for, project, description), task settings on the right (due date, **priority**, repeat, collaborators, Task with a form). On a phone it is a panel at the bottom (on the Tasks tab, the red **+** opens it).
  - The task is for **Myself** by default; choose **Others** and type part of a name to find people.
  - The toolbar at the bottom: **More**, **Formatting** (bold, italic, strikethrough, lists), **Emoji**, **@mention** (people you mention become collaborators), **Attach a file or paste an image**, **Due date** and **Repeat**. If Pico Bot is on for you, **✨** writes the description from the task's name.
  - Type **/** in the description for the command menu.
  - Collaborators are set beside **Create task**.
- **Task with a type (form):** for task types IT set up with their own fields.
- **Working on a task:** **Start**, then **Complete** (the type may ask for a note or a file), or tick it on any list. You can mark it **Blocked** with the reason, and add comments, files, subtasks and followers.
- **My Tasks:** Overdue, Recently assigned, Today, Upcoming, Later and your own sections, as a list, board or calendar.
- **Change a task in the table:** click a cell. Name, due date, assignee, priority, collaborators and tags change in place (where you may change the task). Created by can't be changed. On the board, columns have a coloured top edge and a count, and each card shows who it's for, the due date and the priority.
- **Projects:** private, department or everyone; List, Board, Calendar, Timeline and Overview. Start blank or from a template; **Share** a project or a task.
- **Periodic tasks** repeat (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or some days after completion) and are marked ↻.
- **Managers** see the tasks of everyone below them.
- **Separate from approvals:** completing a task never approves a request.

### Meetings
- **Notifications:** every meeting message is in your **Inbox** (and the bell) under **Meetings**: invitations, points and MOM sent to you to approve or ready to read, changes and cancellations, deadline reminders and action follow-ups.
- **Meeting Overview on Home:** Home has **Approvals**, **Task Management** and **Meetings** at the top. Meetings shows the cards (upcoming, to approve, MOM to write, your open actions), **Needs attention** (points or MOM late or due soon, meetings not started or still running, overdue actions, actions to confirm), Coming up, Waiting for my approval, Done to confirm, **Insights**, and for a period the on-time figures, by type and department, and the register. (There is no separate Meeting dashboard page any more.)
- **Who does what:** each meeting has one **Meeting Owner** (approves the points and the MOM) and one **Coordinator** (schedules it, adds the points, marks attendance, writes the MOM). They can be the same person; then sending counts as approved.
- **Schedule a meeting:** Meetings → **Schedule a meeting**. Choose the type, date and times, the Owner, Coordinator, participants and departments by typing names. Optional: **Points due by** and **MOM due by** (the Coordinator is reminded), and what happens if the points aren't approved in time.
- **Points:** the Coordinator presses **Send to Meeting Owner**; the Owner presses **Approve points** or **Return** with what to change. Approved points go to every participant.
- **Invitees** answer **Attend**, **Maybe** or **Can't attend** (with a reason).
- **Enter the MOM (before or after the meeting):** **Enter MOM**, then tick **Started on time** (if not, enter when it started), **Meeting was extended** (enter when it ended; the extra minutes are shown, and in the meeting details afterwards), **Points shared in time** and **MOM in time** (ticks, never worked out). Tick each person who was present (no tick = absent). The meeting's date and times can be edited until the MOM goes to the Owner. Then note note the discussion point by point, the decisions, the **action items** (one responsible person, priority, due date) and **open points**, then **Send MOM to Meeting Owner**.
- **When the Owner approves the MOM** each action item becomes a task for its owner, linked to the meeting.
- **Follow-ups:** the Owner or Coordinator presses **Confirm done** on a completed action, or **Send back** with what is still needed (the task reopens). **Schedule follow-up meeting** carries open actions and open points into the next meeting. **Close meeting** once every action is confirmed or carried forward.
- **Meeting analytics** (Analytics → Meeting analytics): on time, attendance, approvals and action follow-up for the meetings your scope covers: yours, your whole team below you, the departments you head, or everything (IT; ED and MD can switch to all meetings).
- **Who sees a meeting:** the people on it, heads of its departments (not Management-only meetings), the Management group (ED, MD) and IT.

### Tasks: the popup and activity
- **Opening a task** from a list shows it in a popup with all its actions; × , Esc or a click outside closes it, **Open full page** opens its page.
- **Comments / All activity:** Comments is the thread (reply, like, pin); All activity is the full history, oldest or newest first.
- **Choosing a person** anywhere: type a name, code or department; each choice shows **Name - Employee code** and the department below. Lists open where they fit on the screen and close with ×, Esc or a click outside.

### Information
- **New information:** choose the type, write the subject and message, choose recipients, then **Publish** (or **Save draft**).
- **Recipients are fixed at publishing:** people who join a department later don't receive it.
- **Information inbox:** opening an item marks it read. If it asks for acknowledgement, press **Acknowledge**.
- **Sent by me:** shows who read and who acknowledged.

### AI Assistant (Pico Bot)
Only if IT turned it on for you. Tap the round Pico Bot button: its window grows out of the button and fills about three quarters of the screen; close or minimise it and it shrinks back into the button. Ask questions about your approvals, tasks and, if allowed, the ARS Salesforce. Your chats are listed under it in the sidebar. Pico Bot gives real numbers or says it can't; it never approves or changes anything. Your question and the data needed to answer it go to the AI provider IT chose; check important figures, and don't paste passwords or other people's personal details (see the privacy policy and terms).

### Calendar
Month, week, day or **Agenda** (the period as a list). The **Show** dropdown picks what is on the calendar: Approvals, Tasks, Information, Needed by and Personal, each ticked when shown, with how many entries it has; choose one to hide or show it. Each kind has its own coloured stripe beside its label. **Add a personal entry** at the bottom; open an entry to edit or delete it. "From Google" marks entries from your Google Calendar.

### Analytics
One page with tabs. You see only the dashboards for your modules and role.
- **Choosing what you see:** whose numbers (your requests, your approvals, **your tasks**, your team, your department, or everyone for IT), the period and the comparison, then **Apply**.
- **"How these are worked out"** explains every number.
- **A figure shown as "not available"** isn't recorded by the app yet; it is never guessed.

### Profile
Open it from the account menu (your name, top right). It has five tabs:
- **Profile:** your details, photo and About me, and what IT holds about you (manager, head of department, team, access, modules). Only IT can change your details.
- **Notifications:** choose, kind by kind, what reaches you in the app, by email and on WhatsApp; the morning summary; **Do not disturb** (pause, quiet hours, days off). Approvals waiting for you and security messages always come. The notices themselves are in the **Inbox**.
- **Out of office:** name someone to approve for you between two dates (up to 90 days, India dates, whole days). They see what is already waiting and anything new. Both names are recorded on every decision.
- **Sign-in & devices:** how you sign in, your last 10 sign-ins, and **Sign out everywhere** if a phone or laptop was lost.
- **Calendar:** connect your own Google Calendar.
  - Your approvals and tasks with due times, information marked for calendars and your personal entries then appear in Google, and your Google events appear in ARS. Changes follow both ways.
  - **Sync now** runs it at once; **Review conflicts** asks which version to keep; **Disconnect** stops it (optionally removing the events ARS added).
  - A calendar change never approves, completes or acknowledges anything: do that in ARS.

If you get a *new sign-in* alert that wasn't you, use its **This wasn't me** link. Your account is signed out everywhere and locked until IT unlocks it.

### Signing out after no activity
- Two minutes before, the app asks "Still there?": choose **Stay signed in**.
- Typing, clicking or scrolling counts as activity.

### Help
**Help** in the account menu: how-tos and what each status means. **Take the tour** shows the app again. If something goes wrong, use **Copy details for IT** and paste it in your message to IT. It contains a reference IT can look up, and nothing private.

## 2. Approvers
**Waiting for me** lists everything that needs you, oldest first, with due times (*Overdue* in red). Open a request, read it, then:
- **Approve.**
- **Ask for changes:** say what to change.
- **Put on hold:** the time on hold doesn't count against your response time.
- **Reject:** give a reason. It asks before it rejects; rejection is final.

After a decision, **Next waiting for you** opens the next one. Budget warnings show right above the decision buttons.

**Work tasks** (marked *To do*) are things to do rather than approve, e.g. raise a PO. Do the work, write what you did, and press **Mark as done**, or **Ask for changes** if something is missing.

**FYI** messages need nothing from you.

**Task priority** everywhere: Critical, Most Important, Important or Normal. A task assigned to you always opens from its email link, even without the Task module.

**Work task levels** (in New request or an approval type): choose **Work task**, then **Task priority** and an optional **Task due date**. The task reaches the person's Task Management with them when the level starts; tasks raised from a request are listed in its **Work tasks** card. **Linked requests**: type a number or title and pick a request you can see. **Download PDF** gives the request as it looks on screen.

**Follow-ups card** (request page): every follow-up raised from the request with its status and who has it. **Add a follow-up task** in the Work tasks card gives someone a task linked to the request (Task module needed).

**Raise a follow-up** (on an approved request): for a New request one, it opens New request with the details copied and links the new request to the old one; for a template one, choose the type first. A page you don't have access to says so and names it; ask IT if you need it.

**Approve several at once:** on Waiting for me, tick the straightforward approvals (up to 25), press **Review**, check the list, add a comment if you like, and **Yes, approve**. Each one is approved on its own and you see the result for each. Work tasks and requests on hold aren't offered; open them one by one.

**Heads of department:** if IT switches it on, a short email every Monday morning sums up your department's requests from the past week (raised, approved, rejected, still waiting, overdue).

If you don't act in time: you get a **due soon** heads-up first, then reminders and escalations as IT set them up (Admin → Reminders & escalations).

## 3. IT team (Admin space)
Switch to it from the account menu → **Switch space**.
- **Overview, Needs attention, Analytics, Reports, Insights, Dashboards, Workload:** how approvals are flowing; CSV downloads.
- **Records:** every approval request, task and information item, with filters; requests show their **Amount** (also in the CSV), or *Hidden* when the amount field is confidential and you may not see it.
- **Users & roles:** tick several people (or Select all on this page) to Re-invite, Send set-password link, Deactivate or Activate them together, or press **Modules** to give modules, take them away, or make the chosen ones their only modules (your own access and other IT Admins' are left as they are, unless you are the Super Admin). Every new person, added here or by import, is emailed a welcome with a set-password link valid 12 hours. Add people, set department, designation, reporting manager, roles, modules and sign-in method (Google, User ID and password, or Both). **Re-invite** emails their User ID and a set-password link. Deactivate leavers: *Deactivate user* asks to confirm, then signs them out everywhere. *Hand over* moves someone's pending approvals to another person.
- **Departments:** departments (with HOD) and designations. Tick **Approver** on designations of Manager and above. Only approver designations can be chosen to approve.
- **Approver groups:** e.g. Finance team, for "any one of the group".
- **Approval types & workflows:** each approval type has:
  - **the form**, built in the Form Designer: add fields from a library, drag them into order (or ↑/↓), duplicate, remove (with Undo). Fields can show "only when" a rule is true. Each option of a Dropdown, Radio buttons or Multi-select field can have a **colour**; the value then shows as a coloured pill everywhere. A choice field with the key **`priority`** becomes the request's **Priority** (shown beside the title, in the lists' Priority column, sortable and groupable in your option order);
  - **the levels**, built in the Workflow Designer: a map of the flow above the levels (click a box to open that level); levels fold, move (drag or Alt+↑/↓), duplicate and remove (with Undo). Each level says what needs fixing before you save.
    - one step, parallel steps, or a **DoA matrix** (amount bands, with the levels each band needs);
    - for parallel steps where **any one** or **any N** is enough, **If one branch rejects**: end the request (the default), or end it only when the required number can no longer be reached;
    - **choices** (if / otherwise), whose rules use the same editor as the form rules (field, test, value; **all** or **any** of several rows; dates compare as dates);
  - **who approves:** specific people, the requester's manager or manager's manager, a department HOD, a group, a role, a person picked on the form, **everyone with a designation** (optionally only in the requester's department), or, for work and FYI only, **the requester**;
  - **the kind of step:** *Approval*, *Work task* or *FYI*;
  - **per step:** a response time, and what to do if nobody resolves (stop and tell IT, or skip).

  Changes **save into the draft by themselves** a few seconds after you stop; **Save draft** saves at once. Nothing is live until you **Publish**. If a colleague saved or published in the meantime, you're told and nothing of theirs is overwritten. Check a draft with **Test mode** before publishing. Untick *Open for new requests* to archive a type. **Remove approval type** works only if it was never used.
- **Custom approval:** the fields and options for **New request**.
- **May finish without an approver** (approval type settings, predefined types only): if every level of a request is skipped by its rules, a ticked type may end Approved; otherwise the request goes to the requester's HOD (or to IT if there is no HOD). A request never ends approved by nobody unless IT ticked this.
- **Asks first:** deactivating a group, department or designation, closing a type for new requests and saving working days each ask you to confirm.
- **Approval authority (DoA)** and **Budgets.**
- **Task set-up:** Task types and **Task settings** (department holidays, morning summary, repeating tasks). **Information types.** **Meeting settings:** meeting types with default points, and the Management group (ED, MD) who see every meeting.
- **Reminders & escalations:** rules for pending approvals and open tasks, escalation levels and wording. **Test** sends nothing.
- **AI Analytics** and **AI Rules** (what Pico Bot may answer).
- **Notifications:** send a notification to people, departments, roles, heads of department, managers or everyone active.
  - Choose its category (Announcement, Management notice, System notice, Important alert), its priority and the page it opens.
  - **Count who it reaches** first; **Send** asks to confirm. It goes to their Inbox and their registered devices, and is in the audit log.
  - **History** shows how many were accepted by the push services, are still waiting or failed. **Devices** lists every registered device; the push address is never shown.
- **Working calendar:** working days and holidays (used for due times).
- **Users & roles:** filter by **Department**, Status and Module; the **⋮** menu at the end of each row has Re-invite, Send set-password link (or reset), Deactivate / Activate user and Open user (each asks first).
- **Import data:** Excel templates (.xlsx) for users, departments, designations, groups and holidays: fill the first sheet (every column is Text, so IDs and phone numbers stay as typed; the "How to fill" sheet explains each column) and upload it as it is. A CSV with commas, semicolons or tabs also works. Check first, then import; the problem list downloads as Excel too. In the users file, **modules** gives module access (APPROVAL;TASK;INFORMATION;AI or ALL; empty keeps an existing person's access and gives a new person Approval, Task and Information). Order for a new setup: designations, departments (leave HOD empty the first time), users, then departments again with the HODs.
- **Stuck requests:** if a level finds nobody (e.g. a department has no HOD), IT gets a message and the request shows **Stuck: needs IT**. You can give the step to people, skip it, or **Try again** after fixing the master data. Your reason is shown on the timeline and to the requester. Nobody can skip or route a step of their **own** request (ask another IT person), and a request is never approved when every approval level was skipped for want of an approver: it stops as Stuck instead.

## 4. Super Admin
- **Phone & desktop notifications:** Settings → **Generate keys** once (no outside account is needed); choose what a lock screen shows (title, full message or only "New in ARS CMS"). **Daily session** (Sign-in security): phones and the app until midnight (default), everyone, or off.
- **Providers & security:** the settings console: categories down the side, a search across every setting, a mark on anything not saved yet. Email (Resend), WhatsApp (CEOITBox, including the webhook secret), sign-in security, idle sign-out, escalation levels, the maintenance notice (General). Secret values are stored encrypted. **General → Weekly HOD report** switches the Monday email to heads of department on or off. **Backup:** off-site copies to an S3-compatible bucket (endpoint, region, bucket, keys); copies are always encrypted first.
- **Integrations → Google Calendar:** set up the Google OAuth client, policies and modules; **Test configuration**, then **Enable**; watch health, failed syncs (**Retry**), connected people (**Revoke**, **Full resync**) and conflicts. Maintenance mode pauses syncing. Step by step: deployment guide, Part Q.
- **AI settings, Salesforce Settings, AI Details** (data sources, reports, activity history).
- **Page access:** hide pages, or make them view-only, for everyone, a department or a person (the most specific rule wins).
- **WhatsApp templates:** write, submit to Meta, sync status.
- **Requests:** every request; **Delete** with a reason (the people waiting are told). Tick several (or the header box for the page) and **Delete selected** to delete them with one reason; each is logged on its own, and one under a legal hold is skipped.
- **Automation:** every background job and its last run. A step that partly failed (for example a template sync) shows as failed, not OK; an unconfirmed send shows *may have been sent*. See `docs/AUTOMATION_INVENTORY_AND_RECOVERY.md`.
- **Retention & holds**, **Import data**, **Demo data** (type **LOAD DEMO** to load it; it is refused on a database that already has many real people).
- **Home tiles:** **Origin lock** (on when `ORIGIN_SECRET` is set on the host) and **WhatsApp templates still to switch on**.
- **Google Drive backup** (Settings): a service-account key and a shared-drive folder; **Test** checks access without uploading. Each day the encrypted backup is copied there and the newest copies are kept.
- **Security records** (sign-in attempts, WhatsApp codes, ended sessions, used links) are deleted after 90 days by a background step; the audit log is kept.
- **Break-glass:** while the emergency window is open from the server, the break-glass account signs in with its password alone; you are alerted each time.
- **Asks first:** removing a provider key and hiding a page for everyone ask you to confirm.
- **Message deliveries:** every email and WhatsApp, with retries and (with the webhook) DELIVERED and READ.
- **Audit log:** every change; **Download CSV** for a date range.
- **Sign-in history:** every sign-in attempt, with the reason it failed.
- **Break-glass:** every change made with the break-glass account is recorded and alerts the Super Admins and the holder.
- **Overview → Backups:** the last backup (checksum, encrypted when `BACKUP_ENCRYPTION_KEY` is set, and the off-site copy's status); **Back up now**. `/api/health/deep` reports the database, the last backup's age and degraded checks for monitoring. How to restore: deployment guide, Part P, and `docs/BACKUP_RESTORE_AND_ROLLBACK.md`.
