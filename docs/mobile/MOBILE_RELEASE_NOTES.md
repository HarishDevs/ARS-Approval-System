# Mobile release notes

## v1.43.0 (2 Nov 2026): the phone layout

For everyone using ARS-CMS on a phone (or any window narrower than 768 px). A
computer looks and works exactly as before.

- **Bottom bar:** Home, Approvals, Tasks, Inbox, More, with what waits for you and what you haven't read.
- **Home:** what needs you in one line; four counts; New request, New task, Search and Ask AI; the approvals waiting for you; your tasks due today; your requests in progress.
- **Approvals:** Waiting for me, My requests, Approved and Drafts as cards with the request number, who raised it and their department, the amount, who has it, when it was submitted, how long it has waited, Overdue or Due soon, and its status.
- **Deciding:** on a request, Approve, Return, Hold and Reject stay at the bottom; each asks for a comment where one is needed, and Reject asks once more.
- **Tasks:** Today, Upcoming, Overdue, Completed and Assigned by me; a task from an approval shows its request number; the red + adds a task.
- **Inbox:** All, Approvals, Tasks, Information and Mentions.
- **More:** your profile, every other page you can open, and Sign out.
- **Installed app:** a light-grey splash; long-press the icon for Approvals, Tasks and Inbox.

Nothing new is stored about you except one cookie that remembers the layout (see the
privacy policy). No new permissions: you see exactly what you saw before.

Not in this release: phone-specific screens for the task page, Calendar, Analytics,
AI chat and forms (they work in the phone layout as they are), biometric sign-in and
working offline.
